About VaxaGuard

VaxaGuard is a modern, privacy-first, community-oriented full-stack vaccination and immunization management system built as an individual BTech engineering project. Developed using Node.js, Express, MongoDB Atlas, and modern frontend technologies, it bridges the gap between individual family records and institutional community healthcare.
